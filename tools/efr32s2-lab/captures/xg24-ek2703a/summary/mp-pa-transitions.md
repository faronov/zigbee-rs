# RAIL MP-PA (10 dBm) transitions (EK2703A, GSDK 4.5.0 RAILtest, channel 26)

Decoded from `raw/sampler.tar.xz` with the same sampler as the BRD4187A capture
(`../xg24-brd4187a/railtest-sampler/app.c`): one 30-byte RAIL transmission
(`setTxTransitions idle idle`, TX FIFO refilled before each run), seven
registers sampled every ~12 us at 39 MHz HCLK, a row per change. Times from
`RAIL_StartTx`; `setPower` in deci-dBm.

Compared with the HP PA: no `MODEM.PADEBUG` manual selection (the level comes
from `RAMPLEV` through the ramp), `RAC.TX` pre-stage 0xC001_3001 (no 20 dBm
`VMID`), ramp 0xC029_F003 and steady 0xC029_C001 (`TXPAEN10DBM`,
`TXPAEN10DBMPREDRV`); `PACTRL` holds `TXPASLICERST` with
`TXPASELPREDRVREGVDDPA` in the pre-stage and only the latter in TX. The split
between pre-stage and ramp varies with the level (36/12 us at 0 and 10 dBm,
12/36 us at -30 dBm); the total is the same.
